## What the kernel does

The kernel in `kernel/equations.py` computes mathematical scores against data you provide. The output is a ranking — a number — produced by formulas documented openly in `MATHEMATICS.md`. The kernel makes no claim about what you should do with that ranking, what real-world opportunities it should be applied to, or whether any specific protocol will produce the returns it advertises.

## Sample data is illustrative

The fixtures in `fixtures/` are **synthetic samples**. They use names like "Sample · stable-asset low-risk wrapper" and are designed to demonstrate the kernel's behavior, not to score real protocols. Any resemblance to operational protocols is purely structural — the numbers in the fixtures are author-chosen to illustrate the math, not actual market data.
